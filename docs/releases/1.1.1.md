# WaveSink 1.1.1

A fix-only release on top of 1.1. Nothing to do when upgrading: your routing,
levels and profiles are untouched.

## Fixed in 1.1.1

- **The window is no longer blank on WebKitGTK 2.54+.** 2.54 stopped
  compositing transparent windows on some GPU stacks (seen on NVIDIA with
  KWin), so WaveSink came up as an empty frame. On 2.54 and later the window
  now runs opaque, which fills in its rounded corners; anything older keeps the
  transparent, rounded window exactly as before.
- **The AppImage starts where permissions are enforced.** Its contents were
  packed readable only by root, so `AppRun` was refused under a sandbox such as
  firejail and the app exited without a window. The payload is packed
  world-readable now.
- **Apps:** with two or more channels playing, the second channel's heading sat
  flush against the card above it instead of keeping its gap.

### Packages

`.deb`, `.rpm`, Arch (`.pkg.tar.zst`), AppImage and a Nix flake, with
